CRITICAL MARKS REVISION / ADMIN APPROVAL POLICY
This is a required workflow, not optional:
1. Teacher may enter initial marks and save an initial grading record.
2. BEFORE the student's result is published, any subsequent modification of saved marks requires an explicit revision request to Admin. Teacher provides student, course/exam, old marks, proposed new marks, and written justification (e.g. recheck).
3. Pending revision MUST NOT alter the effective current marks. Admin sees request and approves/rejects with decision note. Approved revision applies the new marks; rejected request retains old marks.
4. Maintain a transparent audit history (request ID, teacher, student, exam, old/new values, reason, decision, decision timestamp). Provide screens for Teacher "My Revision Requests" and Admin "Pending Approvals".
5. Prevent publication of an affected result while a marks revision is pending. Teacher sees clear hold status. Once admin resolves it, publishing may continue according to configured release conditions.
6. After results are published, standard teacher edits are locked. Show locked state. Do not add a hidden bypass that silently edits grades. A real post-publication correction process would require separate governance and is out of scope for this prototype.
